SILQ is a law firm in Thailand for people who need clear answers. Our clients are business owners, investors, families and companies doing business here, many of them from overseas. They come to us when they are starting something new, buying or building property, bringing in staff, dealing with a regulator, or sorting out a problem that has already started.

We are a team of Thai and international lawyers. We work in English and Thai, with offices on the Eastern Seaboard at Pratamnak Hill and in Phuket, and we advise clients across the country.

How we work

Thai law can look straightforward on paper and work very differently in practice. Our job is to explain how it actually works for your situation, what the options are, what each one costs and risks, and what we would do in your position. Then we do the work: drafting, filing, negotiating and, where it is needed, representing you before the authorities and the courts.

  • Plain language. We explain the law in terms you can act on, in English or Thai.
  • Straight answers. If something cannot be done lawfully, we say so, and we look for a route that can.
  • Clear costs. We agree the scope and fees before we start, and tell you early if anything changes.
  • Quick replies. We aim to answer every enquiry within one business day.

What we help with
